About

Hi, I'm Gianpaolo. By day I'm a developer, in the evening I'm dad to a 3-year-old — and later still, once the house finally falls asleep, I do what I like best: building games and small apps.

Hence the name: After Bedtime Projects. No team, no office — just a couple of free hours a night and the will to actually finish what I start.

In this arcade you'll find "Vita da DS", a narrative football management game I work on in my spare time, and "Broodle", an app for planning game nights that I use myself with my own friends.
